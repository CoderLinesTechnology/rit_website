# Open items and external dependencies

Things that are not finished, not verified, or that only the Foundation (or a third party) can
decide or supply. None of them is a code defect; each blocks or limits going live in some way.
"Where to set it" says where in the system the answer goes once known.

**Priority:** *Blocker* = do before public launch. *Soon* = shortly after or alongside launch.
*Optional* = only if the Foundation wants it.

| # | Item | Why it matters | Who decides / provides | Where to set it | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hosting account** (cPanel plan with PHP 8.2+, MySQL/MariaDB, Terminal or SSH, cron) | The site cannot go live without it. The document-root choice (layout A, B or C) depends on what the host allows | Foundation (purchase or confirm the plan) | [DEPLOYMENT.md](DEPLOYMENT.md) steps 1 to 7 | Blocker |
| 2 | **DNS** for `ritfoundationglobal.com` and `www` pointing at the hosting account | Visitors cannot reach the site; AutoSSL needs working DNS | Whoever controls the domain registrar | Registrar's DNS panel; then DEPLOYMENT.md step 11 | Blocker |
| 3 | **SSL certificate** (AutoSSL) for both host names | Sign-in and forms must be encrypted. The site sends HSTS once HTTPS is on, so a broken certificate blocks visitors | Host / Foundation | cPanel SSL/TLS Status; `.env` `APP_URL`, `APP_FORCE_HTTPS` | Blocker |
| 4 | **Database and cPanel credentials**; strong unique passwords for the app and privileged DB users | Required for `.env`; not provided to the developers | Foundation / host | `.env` `DB_*` (DEPLOYMENT.md steps 2 and 5) | Blocker |
| 5 | **SMTP mailbox credentials** (for example `no-reply@ritfoundationglobal.com`) and **SPF and DKIM** records | Without it no notifications, confirmation emails or password resets are sent (messages are marked "skipped"); poor SPF/DKIM sends mail to spam | Foundation / host | `.env` `MAIL_*`; cPanel Email Accounts and Email Deliverability; staff recipient under Settings > Email notifications (DEPLOYMENT.md step 10) | Blocker |
| 6 | **First administrator and staff accounts**; who holds which role | No account is shipped | Foundation nominates people | `php scripts/create-admin.php` or `/setup`; Admin > Administrators | Blocker |
| 7 | **Data Protection Commission registration** (Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012, Act 843) | The Foundation collects personal data (including from under-18s) and is a data controller | Foundation (legal / board) | Outside the system; record the registration number and mention it in the privacy notice (Settings > Privacy & terms) | Blocker |
| 8 | **Legal review of the privacy notice and terms** | The texts in the system are drafts written by the developers, not legal advice | Foundation with a legal adviser | Settings > Website content > Privacy & terms; then tick "reviewed" (this clears the dashboard reminder; it does not change what visitors see) | Blocker |
| 9 | **Named data processors** (hosting provider, email provider, Google Maps if used, payment provider later) | The privacy notice must tell people who receives their data | Foundation | Privacy notice text; [COMPLIANCE.md](COMPLIANCE.md) "Processors" | Blocker |
